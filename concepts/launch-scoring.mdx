When a new token launches on Robinhood Chain, the window to act is short. VAG's launch scoring pipeline runs automatically the moment a new token is detected, giving you an AI-generated viability score and a legitimacy fingerprint before most traders have even heard of the project. Over time, VAG records day-7 outcomes for every scored launch — building a ground-truth dataset that shows which signals actually separate winners from duds.

## The Scoring Pipeline

Every launch moves through four stages from detection to outcome:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Alert">
    The **rh-launch-bot** detects a new token deployment on Robinhood Chain and fires an alert. This is the trigger for all downstream scoring — nothing is scored until the on-chain event is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Viability Score">
    An AI viability score from **0 to 100** is assigned at launch time. The score synthesizes signals from the project's description, developer wallet history, social presence, and team information — the same signals captured by the legitimacy fingerprint. Higher scores indicate stronger early-stage quality signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legitimacy Fingerprint">
    Eight structured checks run simultaneously with scoring to surface red flags and quality signals. See the [Legitimacy Fingerprint](/concepts/legitimacy-fingerprint) page for a full breakdown of what each check verifies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day-7 Outcome Check">
    Seven days after launch, VAG records the outcome:
    - **Good** — the token graduated or achieved 3× or more of its launch market cap
    - **Dud** — everything else

    This binary outcome is stored against the launch's viability score, building the win-rate dataset you see on the Launches page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the AI Viability Score Considers

The viability score is computed from signals that are available at the moment of launch — no hindsight, no post-launch data. The primary inputs mirror the fingerprint checks:

| Signal | What It Captures |
|---|---|
| Project description quality | Is the description genuine, specific, and original? |
| Developer wallet age | Has the deployer wallet been active long enough to suggest a real team? |
| Social presence | Are Twitter/X and other social accounts linked and populated? |
| Team signals | Is team information provided, with verifiable accounts? |
| Website | Does the project have a live web presence? |

The score is a composite — a project that passes all checks strongly will score higher than one that passes only a few, and the relative weight of each signal is learned from the historical outcome dataset.

## Reading the Win-Rate Chart

On the [Launches page](/features/launch-feed), the **win-rate-by-viability-bucket** chart groups all recorded launches into score ranges and shows the percentage of each bucket that achieved a "good" outcome at day 7. Use this chart to calibrate how much weight to give a score when you're evaluating a new alert:

- Buckets with consistently high win rates tell you the score is a meaningful predictor in that range
- Buckets with low or flat win rates suggest the score alone isn't enough — dig into the fingerprint

## Current Dataset

VAG has recorded **1,000 day-7 outcomes** to date. The win-rate chart and per-check analysis on the Launches page are all derived from this growing dataset. As more outcomes are recorded, the signal-to-noise ratio in the buckets improves.
