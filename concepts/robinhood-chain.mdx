Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum Layer 2 network launched by Robinhood Markets ($HOOD), the retail brokerage platform with 28.5 million funded customers and $369 billion in platform assets. VAG tracks Robinhood Chain in real time because it has emerged as a primary destination for Virtuals token launches and tokenized real-world assets — and its on-chain activity metrics now rival or exceed those of Base, Coinbase's competing L2.

VAG tracks both Robinhood Chain and Base head-to-head so you can see where activity, liquidity, and launch volume are concentrating. The comparison tells a different story depending on which metric you look at.

Base holds a significant lead in total value secured and DeFi TVL, reflecting its longer history and Coinbase's 8.2 million monthly transacting users. However, Robinhood Chain is growing faster — its value secured rose 6.3% in the past seven days versus Base's 0.7% — and it now leads Base on active daily addresses, daily transactions, DEX volume, app fees, and Virtuals launch count.
