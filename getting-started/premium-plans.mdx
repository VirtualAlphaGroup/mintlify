## How to Subscribe

1. Go to [virtualalphagroup.com/pricing](https://virtualalphagroup.com/pricing)
2. Click the plan you want to activate
3. Complete checkout securely via Stripe

Stripe handles the entire payment flow. VAG never sees your card number or billing details — your payment information is stored and processed exclusively by Stripe.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
  Yes. Basic is a month-to-month subscription with no contracts or cancellation fees. You can cancel at any time from your [subscription management page](/premium/manage-subscription), and your access remains active through the end of your current billing period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When will Pro and Alpha be available?">
  Pro and Alpha are coming soon. Keep an eye on the [pricing page](https://virtualalphagroup.com/pricing) for announcements — availability and launch dates will be posted there first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
  VAG accepts all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Additional payment methods may be available depending on your region.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
  There is no time-limited free trial, but the public VAG platform offers a substantial amount of free data — including chain race metrics, the token launch directory, the launchpad leaderboard, the stock token directory, and the RWA news feed. Premium unlocks the layers on top of that free foundation: live prices, full launch scores, whale tracking, and the Launch Radar feed.
</Accordion>
