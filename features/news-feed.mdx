The VAG news feed is your single destination for real-world asset (RWA) and tokenization coverage. Every headline that matters — from regulatory moves to tokenized stock milestones to Robinhood Chain updates — surfaces automatically in one place. You can find the feed at [virtualalphagroup.com/news](https://virtualalphagroup.com/news).

## What the feed covers

The news feed focuses on the infrastructure and market events reshaping asset ownership on-chain:

- **RWA tokenization** — announcements, protocol launches, and market-cap milestones for tokenized assets
- **Robinhood Chain** — Robinhood and Robinhood Chain headlines also run on the VAG homepage wire so you never miss a platform-level update
- **Regulatory news** — SEC rulings, exemptions, and institutional policy shifts that affect tokenized securities
- **Institutional adoption** — bank and asset-manager moves into tokenization (JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Securitize, and others)
- **Tokenized stock milestones** — holder counts, volume records, and market-cap growth across tokenized equities
