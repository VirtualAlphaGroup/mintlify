The Launch Feed at [virtualalphagroup.com/launches](https://virtualalphagroup.com/launches) gives you a live, scrollable record of every token launch VAG's `rh-launch-bot` has detected on Robinhood Chain. Each row surfaces the token name, launch timestamp, current outcome status, and — for Premium subscribers — an AI viability score and legitimacy fingerprint breakdown, so you can evaluate launches at a glance rather than digging through on-chain data yourself.

## What the Bot Detects

VAG's `rh-launch-bot` monitors every pool-creation event emitted by factory contracts on Robinhood Chain, including launches originating from Virtuals Protocol. The moment a new pool is created, the bot fires an alert and logs the launch to the feed.

## Reading the Feed

The recent launches table shows the last **100 alerted launches**. Each row contains:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Token** | Token name and ticker (e.g. JKER, HOODAI, CANDY) |
| **Time** | Launch time in UTC |
| **Status** | Outcome classification — Pending, Good, or Dud |
| **Viability Score** | AI score assigned at launch time *(Premium)* |
| **Fingerprint** | Legitimacy check results *(Premium)* |

### Status Definitions

- **Pending** — The launch is less than 7 days old; the day-7 outcome has not yet been assessed.
- **Good** — By day 7, the token either graduated (listed on a major DEX with sufficient liquidity) or reached 3× or more of its launch market cap.
- **Dud** — By day 7, the token did not graduate and did not reach the 3× market cap threshold.

VAG has recorded **1,000 outcomes** to date, giving you a growing historical reference for how launches on Robinhood Chain actually perform.

## Score and Fingerprint Methodology

Every alerted launch receives an AI viability score and a legitimacy fingerprint at the time of detection. Seven days later, VAG checks the outcome and records it as Good or Dud. The analytics on the Launches page let you see, over time, whether higher-scored launches actually produce better outcomes.
