The Launchpads page at [virtualalphagroup.com/launchpads](https://virtualalphagroup.com/launchpads) shows you every factory contract that has launched tokens on Robinhood Chain. Rather than piecing together on-chain data yourself, you get a ranked leaderboard with launch counts, DEX trading volume, and each launchpad's own token price and market cap — all in one place.

## How Indexing Works

VAG indexes **pool-creation events** directly from the chain. Every time a factory contract deploys a new token pool, VAG records it and attributes the launch to that originating factory address. Labeled contracts — such as Virtuals Protocol or Uniswap — are identified by name; unlabeled contracts appear by their on-chain address until they are identified and tagged.

## What the Leaderboard Shows

For each factory, the leaderboard surfaces:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Position by launch count |
| **Launchpad** | Contract label (if known) and address |
| **Kind** | Classification — `launchpad`, `DEX`, or `unlabeled` |
| **Launches** | Total number of token launches attributed to this factory |
| **Volume** | DEX-pair trading volume (Dune historical + DexScreener live feed, hourly buckets) |
| **Token** | Native token price and market cap, where applicable |

## Key Notes

**Virtuals Protocol** (`0xfc2e…20a9`) is the primary Virtuals Protocol factory on Robinhood Chain and the source of the launches tracked in the [Launch Feed](/features/launch-feed). It currently has 165 attributed launches and its native token trades at **$0.559** with a **$367.7M market cap**.

**Unlabeled contracts** are factory addresses that have been indexed from pool-creation events but have not yet been identified and labeled. If you recognize an address, the VAG community can help get it tagged.

**Generic DEX factories** — such as the Unknown V3 DEX with 3,547 launches — carry launch counts but are not treated as dedicated launchpads. Volume and token data are attributed where a clear pairing exists.

<Note>
  Volume figures reflect **DEX-pair volume only** (Dune historical data combined with a DexScreener live feed in hourly buckets). Pre-graduation bonding-curve volume — the trading that happens before a token graduates to a DEX — is not yet included. Launches are always attributed to their originating factory contract.
</Note>
