You can scan the full 194-token list, compare on-chain caps and holder counts at a glance, and click any row to drill into a token's individual detail page.

Open [virtualalphagroup.com/stocks](https://virtualalphagroup.com/stocks) in your browser. No login is required to view the directory.

## How the Table Works

The main table lists every tokenized stock with the following columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **#** | Current rank by on-chain cap |
| **Symbol** | Token ticker (e.g. NVDA, TSLA) |
| **Company** | Full issuer name |
| **Price** | Live token price in USD |
| **On-chain cap** | Tokenized supply × price |
| **Holders** | Number of unique wallet addresses holding the token |

Click any column header to sort the table by that field.

## Key Term Definitions

- **On-chain cap** — The tokenized supply of a stock on Robinhood Chain multiplied by its current price. This reflects only the value of tokens that have been issued on-chain, not the total market capitalization of the underlying company.
- **Holder positions** — The sum of all unique wallet addresses holding each token across the entire directory.
- **Actively trading** — Tokens that have recorded recent DEX activity on Robinhood Chain. Tokens with no recent trades still appear in the directory but may show no price data.

## Finding a Specific Stock

Use your browser's built-in page search (**Ctrl+F** / **Cmd+F**) to search by ticker symbol or company name, or scroll through the full 194-token list. The table's sort controls let you reorder by price, on-chain cap, or holder count to surface the tokens most relevant to you.

## How Token Identity Is Verified

Every contract address in the directory is traced back to the official **StockFactory** deployment event on Robinhood Chain. VAG does not list user-deployed or unofficial token contracts — only canonical tokenized equities and ETFs that originate from the StockFactory registry appear here.

## Individual Token Pages

Click any row in the directory to open that token's detail page, where you can view its contract address, a direct Blockscout link, live price, on-chain cap, and holder count. See [Stock Token Detail Pages](/features/stock-token-detail) for a full breakdown of what each detail page shows.
