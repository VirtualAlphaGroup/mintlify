Whale tracking gives you a real-time view of large holder activity across every tokenized equity on Robinhood Chain. Instead of watching prices move without context, you can see *who* is moving supply and how holder distribution is shifting — before that information makes it into the price. Whale tracking is available on the **Basic plan and above**.

## What whale tracking covers

### Large holder movements
See when wallets holding significant supply add to, reduce, or exit their positions across tokenized equities. Movements are surfaced as they land on-chain, not after a delay.

### Holder count changes per token
Track whether a token's holder base is growing or contracting over time. A rising holder count alongside stable prices can signal quiet accumulation; a falling count alongside rising prices may indicate consolidation into fewer wallets.

### Supply concentration among top wallets
Understand what percentage of a token's circulating supply sits in the top 10, 25, and 100 wallets. High concentration is a key risk and opportunity signal for on-chain researchers.
