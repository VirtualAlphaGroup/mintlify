Every token the `rh-launch-bot` detects on Robinhood Chain gets scored immediately at launch — before the market has had a chance to move. VAG then revisits each launch at the 7-day mark and records a concrete outcome. By pairing scores with real outcomes across a growing dataset, you can see which early signals actually correspond to winning tokens rather than relying on gut feel or lagging price action.

## The AI Viability Score

The viability score is assigned at the moment of launch detection. It combines legitimacy fingerprint checks (see below) with broader project signals to produce a single rating that indicates how viable a launch looks at the earliest possible moment.

- **Visibility:** Available to **Premium (Basic+)** subscribers via the [Launch Feed](/features/launch-feed) and [Launch Radar](/premium/launch-radar).
- **Timing:** Scored once at launch; the score does not change after assignment.
- **Purpose:** A higher score signals that more positive indicators were present at launch — it is a starting point for your own research, not a guaranteed outcome.

## The Day-7 Outcome Check

Seven days after each launch, VAG automatically checks the token's status and records one of two outcomes:

- **Good** — The token graduated (achieved a DEX listing with sufficient liquidity) or reached **3× or more** of its launch market cap.
- **Dud** — The token did not graduate and did not reach the 3× market cap threshold within 7 days.

With **1,000 outcomes recorded**, the Launches page at [virtualalphagroup.com/launches](https://virtualalphagroup.com/launches) gives you two analytics views to judge signal quality for yourself.

## How to Interpret the Data

The win rate is calculated per bucket or per check — it tells you what fraction of launches in that group ended up as **Good** outcomes. A few things to keep in mind as you use these analytics:

- **Dataset size matters.** At 1,000 outcomes, patterns are starting to form but individual bucket counts can still be small. Treat low-sample buckets with caution.
- **0% now ≠ 0% forever.** The current cohort reflects a specific slice of launches; as more launches graduate or hit 3×, the win rates will update automatically.
- **Fingerprint edge shows relative value.** Even if absolute win rates are low, a positive edge on a check means that check is a better-than-average predictor within this dataset.
- **Scores are a filter, not a forecast.** A high viability score narrows the field to launches that looked better at inception — it does not guarantee a good outcome.
