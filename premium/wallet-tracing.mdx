Wallet tracing gives you a view behind the curtain of any token launch by mapping the on-chain relationships between developer wallets, funders, and connected addresses. It's a **Pro plan feature** — designed for researchers and serious traders who need to go beyond surface-level launch data and understand *who* is actually behind a project and whether coordinated actors are involved.

## What Wallet Tracing Reveals

### Funder Graphs

VAG traces each developer wallet back to its funding source and renders this as a **funder graph** — a visual map of the chain of capital. This makes it immediately clear when multiple launches share the same funding origin, a strong signal of serial deployment or coordinated activity.

- Identify whether a dev wallet was seeded by a known high-risk address
- See if a single funder has bankrolled five different "independent" launches in the past week
- Track funding chains across multiple hops on-chain

### Wallet Age

The age of a developer wallet is a meaningful signal on its own — and it's also one of the eight checks in the [legitimacy fingerprint](/concepts/legitimacy-fingerprint). Within wallet tracing, you get the full picture: exactly how long the wallet has been active, its transaction history, and any prior launches associated with it.

### Connected Addresses

Beyond the direct funder, wallet tracing surfaces **all other contracts and wallets funded by the same source**. This lets you see the full ecosystem of addresses connected to a developer before you act on any alert.

- Other token launches deployed from connected wallets
- On-chain overlap between seemingly unrelated projects
- Historical patterns of wallet behaviour across chains

## Farm and Cluster Detection

### Farm Detection

A **farm** is a coordinated group of wallets that systematically buys into launches — often to manufacture volume, create artificial holder counts, or set up exit liquidity. VAG's farm detection flags wallets that behave in this pattern, so you can immediately see when a launch's holder base is synthetic rather than organic.

### Cluster Detection

**Cluster detection** goes broader: it groups wallets that consistently move together across multiple launches. When the same set of addresses appears repeatedly as early buyers across different tokens, that's a cluster — and its presence in a new launch is a material signal worth knowing before you make any decision.
