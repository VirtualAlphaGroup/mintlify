Launch Radar is your real-time window into every new token launch on Robinhood Chain. Instead of manually sifting through on-chain noise, you get an instant, structured alert the moment a new launch is detected — complete with an AI viability score and a full legitimacy fingerprint breakdown. That means you can evaluate a project before the crowd has even heard of it, giving you a critical edge in fast-moving markets.

## How It Works

The moment VAG's `rh-launch-bot` detects a new token launch, Launch Radar surfaces it in your feed. Each alert is generated automatically and includes the full AI viability score alongside all eight legitimacy fingerprint checks — so you're never looking at raw contract data, only actionable intelligence.

## What Each Alert Includes

Every Launch Radar alert gives you a complete snapshot of the launch at the moment of detection:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Token name & symbol** | The project's on-chain identity |
| **Launch timestamp** | Exact detection time in UTC |
| **AI viability score** | A composite score assessing the launch's potential |
| **Legitimacy fingerprint** | Pass/fail result for each of the 8 checks (see below) |
| **Status** | `pending` at launch; updated to `good` or `dud` after day 7 |

### Legitimacy Fingerprint Checks

The fingerprint evaluates eight signals at the moment of launch:

| Check | What It Looks At |
|---|---|
| `realDescription` | Whether the project has a meaningful, non-generic description |
| `website` | Whether a live website is linked |
| `socials` | Whether social profiles are present and active |
| `devWalletAge` | How long the developer wallet has been active on-chain |
| `team` | Whether team information is verifiable |
| `xAccount` | Whether a linked X (Twitter) account exists |
| `xPfp` | Whether the X account has a profile picture set |
| `descriptionOriginal` | Whether the description appears original, not copied |

### Day-7 Outcome

Seven days after launch, each alert is updated with an outcome:
- **Good** — the token graduated or achieved 3× or more of its launch market cap
- **Dud** — the token failed to meet either threshold

VAG has recorded over 1,000 outcomes to date, and the dataset continues to grow with every new launch cycle.
