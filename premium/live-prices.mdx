Live prices give you a real-time view of every tokenized equity trading on Robinhood Chain. Free users see static or delayed data; with a **Basic plan or above** you get continuously updating prices, the day's top movers, and on-chain market cap changes as they happen — all sourced directly from on-chain DEX activity.

## What you see with live prices

### Real-time price for every tokenized equity
All 194 tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain display their current on-chain price, updated continuously as new trades settle. You're seeing the same data as the chain — not a delayed feed.

### Top movers
The movers view surfaces the tokens with the biggest price changes over the last 24 hours, both upward and downward. It's the fastest way to identify which equities have active on-chain momentum right now.

### On-chain market cap updates
As prices move, each token's on-chain market capitalisation recalculates automatically. You can track relative size and movement across the full set of tokenized equities at a glance.

## Where to see live prices

Live prices are available on the **Stocks page** at [virtualalphagroup.com/stocks](https://virtualalphagroup.com/stocks). Prices refresh automatically as long as you're logged in as a Premium subscriber — no manual reload required.

Individual token pages also display live prices. Navigate to any tokenized equity and you'll see the current on-chain price, recent price history, and holder intelligence in one view.

## Data source

Prices are sourced from **Blockscout**, pulling on-chain DEX trade data directly from Robinhood Chain. This means prices reflect actual settled trades, not order-book bids or off-chain market feeds.

## What "—" means

If a token's price column shows a dash (—), that token is not actively trading on-chain. No price data is available for that equity regardless of your plan tier.
