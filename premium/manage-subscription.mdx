You manage your VAG Premium subscription directly from the platform. All billing and plan changes are handled through a Stripe-powered portal that you can access at any time — no need to contact support for routine changes.

## Accessing the subscription portal

Go to [virtualalphagroup.com/premium](https://virtualalphagroup.com/premium) and log in if prompted. From there, open the subscription management section to reach the billing portal.

## What you can do in the portal

### Upgrade your plan
When Pro and Alpha plans become available, you can upgrade from Basic directly inside the portal. Your new features activate immediately and Stripe prorates the billing difference.

### Cancel your subscription
You can cancel your Basic subscription at any time — no cancellation fees, no minimum commitment. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period, after which Premium features are removed.

### Update your payment method
Use the Stripe portal to swap in a new card or update expiry details. The change applies to your next billing cycle.

### View billing history
The portal shows a full history of your invoices and payments. You can download individual receipts from here.

## What happens when you cancel

Cancelling stops future charges immediately. You keep full access to your Premium features until the last day of your current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the free tier.

## Payment handling

Stripe processes and stores all payment information. VAG never stores, sees, or has access to your card details at any point.

<Note>
  If you have trouble accessing your subscription portal, reach out to the VAG team via the contact link on [virtualalphagroup.com](https://virtualalphagroup.com). Include the email address associated with your account so the team can locate your subscription quickly.
</Note>
