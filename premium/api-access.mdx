The VAG API gives you programmatic access to the same real-time on-chain intelligence that powers the VAG platform — launches, tokenized equities, chain metrics, and launchpad rankings — delivered directly to your systems. It's built for **teams, quantitative funds, and builders** who need to integrate live Robinhood Chain data into their own workflows, models, or products rather than consuming it through the VAG dashboard.

API access is included in the **Alpha plan ($200/mo, coming soon)**.

## Available Endpoints

The Alpha plan gives you access to four core data surfaces:

| Endpoint | Description |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/launches` | Real-time token launch feed with AI viability scores and legitimacy fingerprints |
| `GET /api/stocks` | Tokenized equity listings — live price, on-chain market cap, and holder count |
| `GET /api/chain-metrics` | Live Robinhood Chain vs Base metrics side by side |
| `GET /api/launchpads` | Factory contract leaderboard ranked by launch counts |

## Authentication

When you subscribe to the Alpha plan, your API key is generated and made available through the **Alpha plan dashboard**. Pass your key in the request header for every API call. Full authentication documentation is provided to active subscribers.

<Warning>
  Do not share your API key with anyone outside your team. If you believe your key has been compromised, contact the VAG team immediately at [virtualalphagroup.com](https://virtualalphagroup.com) — your key can be rotated without interrupting your subscription.
</Warning>

## Rate Limits

Rate limit details are available to active Alpha subscribers. Limits are set to support team-scale usage; if your use case requires higher throughput, reach out to the VAG team to discuss your requirements.

## Custom Watchlists and Team Seats

The Alpha plan includes **custom watchlist management** — configure and save filtered views of the launch feed, stock listings, or wallet activity so your team always starts from the signals that matter most to your strategy. Multiple **team seats** are also included, so your analysts, developers, and risk managers can all access VAG intelligence under a single plan.

## Priority Signals

As an Alpha subscriber, you receive **priority signal delivery** — high-confidence alerts and intelligence updates are pushed to you ahead of Basic and Pro tier users. This ensures that time-sensitive signals reach your models and decision-makers before they move through the broader platform.

  </Step>

</Steps>
